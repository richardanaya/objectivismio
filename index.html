01 / Reason

  • Man's mind
  • Logic
  • Evidence
  • Perception
  • The facts
  • Not feelings
  • No whim

Man's mind is how he knows the world. Perception supplies the material. Logic identifies it without contradiction. Evidence is the check. A feeling can be real and still be no argument. A whim names a wish and skips the work.

02 / Production

  • Create
  • Trade
  • Build
  • Innovate
  • Value
  • Wealth
  • The producer

Production is reason applied to living. You create what was not there, trade value for value, and build what the trade needs. Wealth is that work made real. The producer lives on what he makes, not on permission.

03 / Objective Law

  • Reality
  • A is A
  • Non-contradiction
  • Man's nature
  • Right to life
  • Self-ownership
  • Not granted

Reality is not optional, and it does not contradict itself. A is A. Law states the conditions a rational being needs in order to live. The right to life is the source. Self-ownership is that right, recognized — not handed down.

04 / Rational
self-interest

  • Your life
  • The standard
  • Your mind
  • Your choices
  • Earned reward
  • Your happiness

Your own life is the standard of value. Your mind sets the purpose. Your choices carry it. What you earn is yours because you produced it. Happiness is the state of having succeeded at living — not a debt you owe, and not a claim on anyone else.

05 / The ethic

A philosophy for living on earth.

Objectivism holds the facts a person has to accept if he means to live by his own mind. Reality is objective. Reason is his only means of knowledge. His own life is the standard of value.

It is not a mood, a tribe, or a request for permission. Named in 1957. The requirement is plain: look, judge, act.

What this is

A method for choosing. Four requirements, and they are this page. Think from the facts. Produce value. Deal with other people under objective law. Hold your life as the end.

You do not live for others, and you do not use them as fuel. Trade is the relation between minds that respect that fact.
